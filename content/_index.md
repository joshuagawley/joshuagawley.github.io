Joshua Gawley is a software engineer and composer based in London, currently working at [JPMorganChase](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/). He holds an MEng in Mathematics and Computer Science from the University of Bristol.

His projects include [parallelized fluid simulations](https://github.com/joshuagawley/lattice-boltzmann-hpc) and a [programming language for safe parallel computing](https://github.com/joshuagawley/blues). He has also contributed to various open source projects such as [Meson](https://github.com/mesonbuild/meson), [Simdjson](https://github.com/simdjson/simdjson), and [Typst](https://github.com/typst/typst).

Joshua also enjoys a nascent career as a speaker, having given a [lightning talk](@/talks/peer_pressure.md) at the 2025 ACCU conference about the equal importance of technical skills and people skills in software engineering.

Alongside keyboard smashing, Joshua is also an avid composer and singer, currently singing with [Symbel Choir](https://symbelchoir.co.uk/), and having had works performed by various choirs in Bristol and London, as well as being featured at the [London Festival of Contemporary Church Music](https://lfccm.com/whatson/participants/joshua-gawley/).
